The cockpit

The whole group at a glance, every morning.

  • One screen, three companiesJobs in progress, jobs starting this week, open quotes and vehicles out of service, side by side.
  • Switch company in one clickAll, GSG, Vegamálun or Malbik. The panel remembers your last choice.
  • Problems come to youLow stock and broken vehicles show up on the cockpit before they stop a crew.
Calendar

Every crew knows where to be.

  • Crews as rows, days as columnsEach company has its own calendar, so planning stays clear.
  • Status in colourQuote, scheduled, in progress, done, invoiced. Visible without opening a job.
  • Crew and vehicles on the jobOnly the crews and vehicles of that company can be picked, so nothing gets mixed up.
Quotes

A price in minutes, the same rates for everyone.

  • Car park and road marking calculatorsEnter bays, metres of lines, crossings and symbols. Net, VAT and total appear right away.
  • One price list for the groupRates are set once by the administrator. Every quote and every job uses them.
  • Minimum order built inSmall jobs are topped up to the minimum automatically, so they are never priced at a loss.
Warehouse & fleet

Paint, parts and vehicles under control.

  • Stock goes down by itselfWhen a job is marked done, the materials used are taken off the warehouse once.
  • Reorder warningsEach item has its own minimum. Below it, the cockpit tells you.
  • Fleet status per companyIn use or out of service, why, when it is back, mileage and next inspection.
Finance

See where the money is, and where it is waiting.

  • Revenue per company and per monthCompare GSG, Vegamálun and Malbik on one chart, or look at one company alone.
  • Done, not invoicedA running total of finished work that still needs an invoice, so nothing is forgotten.
  • Margin after materialsRevenue against the materials used on each job. Visible only to administrators.
